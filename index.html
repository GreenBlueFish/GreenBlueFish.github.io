About

Background

I'm a part-time 2A Electrical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo, currently diving deep into neural network internals, neurobiology, and hardware design.

I've built two neural network libraries from scratch in C++ and used them to implement CNNs for map classification, an RNN, an LSTM, and a neural architecture search tool. I'm now studying spiking neural networks through the lens of real neuroscience — reading Neuroscience by Purves et al. and Neuronal Dynamics by Gerstner et al.

On the hardware side I'm learning Verilog, have VHDL experience from coursework, and am joining my university's ASIC design team.
