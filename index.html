About

Background

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo who learns by building things from first principles. That’s led me into neural network internals, neurobiology, and hardware design.

I’ve written two neural network libraries in C++ and used them to recreate architectures like RNNs, LSTMs, and map‑classification models so I could see how they behave at the lowest level. Lately, I’ve been diving into spiking neural networks through real neuroscience, studying texts like Purves’ Neuroscience and Gerstner’s Neuronal Dynamics.

I’ve also designed two computers from scratch in a gate‑logic simulator, picked up VHDL and Verilog, and am joining Waterloo’s ASIC design team.

I follow curiosity wherever it leads, but machine learning—especially neural networks—keeps pulling me deeper.
