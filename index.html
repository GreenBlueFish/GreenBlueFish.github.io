About

Background

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo who loves to build things from first principles. That has led me into learning about neural networks, neuromorphic computing, and ML hardware design.

I’ve written two neural network libraries in C++ and used them to recreate architectures like RNNs, LSTMs, and map‑classification models so I could see how they behave at the lowest level. Lately, I’ve been diving into spiking neural networks (SNN's) and their hardware through textbooks, research papers, and hands on application. I have build a SNN accelerator from scratch and am working with a team to explore SNN's and neuromorphic hardware.

I’ve also designed two computers from scratch in a gate‑logic simulator, picked up VHDL and Verilog, and am apart Waterloo’s ASIC design team.

I follow curiosity wherever it leads, but machine learning—especially neural networks—keeps pulling me deeper.
