STDP 08
Nearest-Neighbor vs. All-to-All Pairing

When a neuron emits multiple spikes, a pairing scheme determines which pre-post spike combinations are counted. All-to-all sums over every combination; nearest-neighbor uses only the most recent spikes from each side. The choice profoundly affects stability, burst sensitivity, and biological plausibility.

TypeImplementation
Cost (NN)O(1)
Cost (A2A)O(N²)
Stability (NN)Good
Stability (A2A)Poor at high rates

All-to-All Rule

All-to-All
Δw = Σ_f Σ_g W(tpost⁽ᵍ⁾ − tpre⁽ᶠ⁾)

Every presynaptic spike f is paired with every postsynaptic spike g and vice versa. The total weight change is the sum over all pairs, weighted by the STDP window W(Δt).

Under high-frequency bursting, all-to-all STDP can create enormous weight changes because a burst of N spikes generates N² pairs. This can cause runaway potentiation unless multiplicative bounds are also applied.

Nearest-Neighbor Rule

Nearest-Neighbor (Symmetric Variant)
At post-spike tpost: Δw⁺ = W⁺(tpost − tprelast)
At pre-spike tpre: Δw⁻ = W⁻(tpostlast − tpre)

Only the immediately preceding spike from the other neuron is counted. Two sub-variants:

VariantPost-spike pairingPre-spike pairingNotes
Symmetric NNLast pre-spikeLast post-spikeMost common; biologically motivated
Reduced NNLast spike of eitherLast spike of eitherMost conservative; fewest updates

Comparison

AspectAll-to-AllNearest-Neighbor

Burst sensitivityHigh — bursts produce many pairsLow — only most recent spike counts

Common useLow-rate regimes, theoretical workNetwork simulations, hardware, high-rate

Advantages

  • All-to-all captures full spike-train statistics for theoretical analysis
  • Nearest-neighbor is biologically consistent with refractory periods
  • Nearest-neighbor is O(1) — ideal for neuromorphic hardware
  • Both can be combined with any STDP window (classical, triplet, etc.)

Limitations

  • All-to-all is unstable at high rates without multiplicative STDP
  • Nearest-neighbor loses burst information that may carry learning signal
  • Neither scheme is definitively confirmed as the biological implementation
  • Different choices give quantitatively different plasticity outcomes
