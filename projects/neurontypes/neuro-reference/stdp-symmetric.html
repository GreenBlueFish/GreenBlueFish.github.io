STDP 06
Symmetric STDP

Both temporal orderings of pre and post spikes produce LTP when the inter-spike interval is small, and LTD when it is large. Temporal proximity matters more than causal direction. Detects synchrony and temporal correlation without directional preference. Found in cerebellar cortex and some neocortical synapses.

Advantages

  • Detects synchrony without requiring causal order — noise robust
  • Natural for binding spatially distributed inputs that co-activate
  • Found in cerebellum — biological support for specific circuits
  • Averages out timing noise; more stable under jittered inputs

Limitations

  • Cannot encode temporal sequences or directional dependencies
  • Fewer experimental benchmarks than asymmetric STDP
  • Less commonly implemented in neuromorphic hardware literature
  • Boundary between LTP and LTD (t_cross) is sensitive to noise
