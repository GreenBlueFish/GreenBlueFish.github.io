MODEL 09
Multi-Compartment Models

Spatially extended neurons modeled as chains or trees of electrically coupled compartments. Each compartment has its own ionic currents and synaptic inputs. The backpropagating action potential (bAP) and local dendritic calcium spikes emerge naturally, enabling location-dependent STDP rules invisible to point-neuron models.

ODEsN×4+
Spike PrecisionRealistic
Dendritic EffectsFull

Governing Equations

SymbolDescriptionTypical
ViMembrane potential of compartment imV
Iion,iSum of all active ionic currents in compartment i (HH-style)pA
gijAxial (inter-compartment) coupling conductance between i and jnS
Isyn,iTotal synaptic current arriving at compartment ipA

Backpropagating Action Potential

bAP Attenuation
VbAP(d) ≈ Vsoma · exp(−d / λ)
SymbolDescriptionTypical
dElectrotonic distance from soma to the synapseμm
λSpace constant — depends on compartment diameter and axial/membrane resistivityμm
VbAP(d)bAP amplitude at distance d — determines NMDA unblockingmV
Why bAP matters: Biological STDP requires the postsynaptic bAP to coincide with the presynaptic EPSP at the synapse. In distal dendrites the bAP is smaller and delayed — meaning the effective STDP time window is location-dependent. A synapse at 300 μm sees a weaker, later bAP and thus has a shifted LTP zone.

Location-Dependent STDP

Synapse LocationbAP AmplitudeSTDP LTP WindowPlasticity Rule
Proximal (< 50 μm)Large (~80% somatic)Wide, standardClassical exponential STDP
Mid-apical (50–200 μm)Moderate (~40%)Narrower, shiftedReduced LTP; more LTD
Distal (> 200 μm)Small (<20%) or zeroRequires local Ca²⁺ spikeAnti-Hebbian or no plasticity

Advantages

  • Full dendritic computation — local calcium spikes, branch-specific plasticity
  • Location-dependent STDP emerges naturally from cable properties
  • bAP dynamics are explicit — enables mechanistic STDP studies
  • Validates against detailed morphological reconstructions (NeuroMorpho)

Limitations

  • Extremely expensive — a realistic pyramidal cell may have 200+ compartments
  • Requires dt ≈ 0.01 ms; numerical stability is demanding
  • Parameter fitting requires extensive experimental data
  • Large-scale network simulations essentially infeasible
