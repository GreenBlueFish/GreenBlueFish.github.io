MODEL 06
FitzHugh-Nagumo Model

A 2D phase-plane reduction of Hodgkin-Huxley capturing the essential topology of excitability — a cubic nullcline for the fast variable and a linear nullcline for the slow recovery variable. Analytically tractable and ideal for theoretical STDP studies, phase-plane visualization, and coupled oscillator dynamics.

ODEs2
Spike PrecisionModerate
Dendritic EffectsNone
Computational CostVery Low
Use CaseTheoretical

Governing Equations

Fast & Slow Variables
dv/dt = v − v³/3 − w + Iext
dw/dt = (1/τ) · (v + a − b·w)
SymbolDescriptionTypical
vFast excitation variable — analogue of membrane voltage (dimensionless)—
wSlow recovery variable — analogue of combined HH gating variables—
τTimescale separation parameter — large τ = slow recovery = bursting tendency12.5
aShape parameter — controls equilibrium position of w-nullcline0.7
bShape parameter — slope of w-nullcline0.8
IextExternal drive — controls excitability regime—

Reset Mechanism

There is no explicit reset. Spikes emerge as large excursions in the (v, w) phase plane when Iext pushes the system past the unstable branch of the cubic v-nullcline:

Phase-plane excitability:
• If Iext is below threshold → stable fixed point (quiescence)
• If Iext crosses threshold → the fixed point loses stability → limit cycle (repetitive spiking)
• The cubic v-nullcline (v − v³/3) creates the characteristic N-shape that allows Type II excitability

Why It Matters for STDP Theory

The FHN model makes STDP analysis tractable because spikes are smooth trajectories with well-defined timing. This allows:

ApplicationBenefit
Phase response curves (PRC)Smooth spikes → differentiable PRC → STDP as phase modulation
Coupled oscillator STDPEntire network analyzed with Kuramoto-style phase equations
Noise analysisAdding ξ(t) to dv/dt gives analytically tractable stochastic STDP
Bifurcation of plasticityParameter sweeps across a, b, τ reveal when STDP stabilizes vs. destabilizes

Advantages

  • Phase-plane geometry is transparent — dynamics are visible
  • Analytically tractable — many closed-form STDP results possible
  • Captures essential HH topology at essentially zero cost
  • Natural for studying synchrony and oscillatory STDP

Limitations

  • Dimensionless — no direct mapping to mV, ms, pA without rescaling
  • Smooth spikes — timing is less precise than threshold-based models
  • Does not reproduce the diversity of firing patterns (LIF beats it here)
