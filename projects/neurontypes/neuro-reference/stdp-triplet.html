STDP 03
Triplet STDP (Pfister-Gerstner Rule)

Extends STDP beyond spike pairs to triplets (pre-post-pre or post-pre-post). Four exponential traces — two fast and two slow — capture interactions between three spikes. Reproduces visual cortex plasticity data far better than the pair rule and recovers BCM-like sliding threshold behavior.

TypeUnsupervised
Traces4
CostLow
StabilityModerate
Bio. PlausibilityHigh

Learning Rule

Triplet Weight Updates
At post-spike: Δw⁺ = A₂⁺·x₁ + A₃⁺·x₁·y₂
At pre-spike: Δw⁻ = A₂⁻·y₁ + A₃⁻·y₁·x₂
Trace ODEs
dx₁/dt = −x₁/τ_{x1} → x₁ ← x₁+1 at pre-spike
dx₂/dt = −x₂/τ_{x2} → x₂ ← x₂+1 at pre-spike
dy₁/dt = −y₁/τ_{y1} → y₁ ← y₁+1 at post-spike
dy₂/dt = −y₂/τ_{y2} → y₂ ← y₂+1 at post-spike
SymbolDescriptionTypical
x₁Fast pre-synaptic trace — pairs with nearest post-spike (like classical STDP)—
x₂Slow pre-synaptic trace — integrates recent pre-spike history over longer window—
y₁Fast post-synaptic trace — pairs with nearest pre-spike—
y₂Slow post-synaptic trace — encodes recent mean post-synaptic activity (≈ BCM θ_M)—
A₂⁺, A₂⁻Pair-interaction amplitudes — equivalent to A₊, A₋ in classical STDP~0.006
A₃⁺, A₃⁻Triplet amplitudes — often 10× larger than pair terms~0.06
τ_{x1}, τ_{y1}Fast trace time constants~17, 19 ms
τ_{x2}, τ_{y2}Slow trace time constants~114, 40 ms

Why Triplets Matter

Post-before-pre-before-post (triplet):
In classical STDP, post-before-pre → LTD. But if there's another post spike right after the pre, this reverses to LTP (the pre-post pair dominates). The triplet rule captures this via the x₁·y₂ term: y₂ is high (recent post activity) and x₁ is high (recent pre spike), creating strong LTP.

BCM connection: y₂ acts like the BCM sliding threshold θ_M — when post activity is high (y₂ large), the A₃⁺ term boosts LTP, equivalent to LTP for firing above θ_M. When post activity is low (y₂ small), the pair term A₂⁻ dominates, giving LTD — equivalent to below-threshold BCM depression.

Advantages

  • Reproduces visual cortex LTP/LTD data (Sjöström et al.) far better than pair rule
  • Recovers BCM sliding threshold behavior without extra assumptions
  • Correctly handles burst interactions — burst pre + single post → strong LTP
  • Still fully local — only four traces per synapse

Limitations

  • Six free parameters — difficult to fit without detailed experimental data
  • Still additive — weight distributions can become unstable
  • Computationally 2× heavier than classical STDP (four traces vs two)
  • Higher-order terms beyond triplets (quadruplets) sometimes matter
