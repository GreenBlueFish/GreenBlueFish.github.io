⬡ Technical Reference

Neuron Models
& STDP Learning Rules

A comprehensive reference covering 10 neuron model formulations and 10 spike-timing-dependent plasticity algorithms — from biophysical equations to computational implementations.

◈ Neuron Models

MODEL 01

Leaky Integrate-and-Fire

The canonical workhorse. Integrates current with a passive leak; fires when threshold is crossed. Analytically tractable and computationally trivial.

1 ODE Very Low Cost High Precision
MODEL 02

Exponential Integrate-and-Fire

Adds an exponential term near threshold to model realistic Na⁺ channel activation dynamics. Produces sharper, more biologically accurate spike initiation.

1 ODE Low Cost Medium Fidelity
MODEL 03

Adaptive Exponential IF

AdEx adds a slow adaptation current to EIF. A single model reproduces regular spiking, bursting, fast spiking, and chattering patterns by tuning four parameters.

2 ODEs Low Cost High Fidelity
MODEL 04

Izhikevich Model

Two coupled equations reproducing ~20 distinct cortical firing patterns. The sweet spot of biological richness and computational efficiency — the most widely used model in SNN research.

2 ODEs Low Cost ~20 Cell Types
MODEL 05

Hodgkin-Huxley

The gold standard. Four coupled ODEs derived from squid axon voltage-clamp data. Full ionic channel dynamics, realistic spike shape, and natural refractoriness.

4+ ODEs High Cost Highest Fidelity
MODEL 06

FitzHugh-Nagumo

A 2D reduction of Hodgkin-Huxley capturing excitability and refractoriness with a cubic nullcline. Analytically tractable; ideal for phase-plane and coupled oscillator analysis.

2 ODEs Very Low Cost Theoretical
MODEL 07

Morris-Lecar

Conductance-based model with Ca²⁺ and K⁺ currents. Class I or Class II excitability from parameter choice. Particularly relevant when calcium dynamics matter for STDP.

2 ODEs Low Cost Ca²⁺ Dynamics
MODEL 08

Spike Response Model

Kernel-based formulation — voltage is a convolution of past spikes with response kernels. No ODE integration required. Natural framework for theoretical STDP analysis.

Kernel-Based Low Cost Analytical
MODEL 09

Multi-Compartment

Spatially extended neurons with dendritic structure. Captures backpropagating action potentials, local calcium spikes, and the location-dependent STDP rules that emerge from dendritic cable properties.

N × 4+ ODEs Very High Cost Full Dendrites
MODEL 10

Stochastic IF

Adds noise to the LIF model via additive white noise or an escape-rate hazard function. Captures trial-to-trial variability, channel noise, and connects STDP to Bayesian inference.

1 ODE + noise Low Cost Probabilistic

◈ STDP Learning Rules

STDP 01

Classical Additive STDP

The foundational rule. Exponential LTP/LTD windows depending on whether pre fires before or after post. Biologically confirmed by Bi & Poo (1998).

Unsupervised 2 traces Very Low Cost
STDP 02

Multiplicative STDP

Weight-dependent updates with soft bounds. Strong synapses resist further potentiation; weak synapses resist depression. Produces stable unimodal weight distributions.

Unsupervised Stable Very Low Cost
STDP 03

Triplet STDP

Pfister-Gerstner rule extending beyond spike pairs to triplets. Recovers BCM-like behavior and fits visual cortex data far better than the classical pair rule.

Unsupervised 4 traces Burst-Sensitive
STDP 04

Voltage-Dependent STDP

Clopath rule: plasticity gated by postsynaptic membrane voltage rather than spikes alone. More biologically realistic — NMDA receptors are voltage-gated coincidence detectors.

Unsupervised Voltage-Gated Medium Cost
STDP 05

Anti-STDP

The sign of the learning window is flipped. Pre-before-post causes LTD; post-before-pre causes LTP. Implements predictive suppression — found in electric fish electrosensory systems.

Anti-Hebbian Predictive Very Low Cost
STDP 06

Symmetric STDP

Both orderings of pre/post spikes produce LTP when closely timed, LTD when distant. Detects synchrony regardless of causal order. Found in cerebellar circuits.

Associative Synchrony Very Low Cost
STDP 07

Reward-Modulated STDP

Three-factor rule: local STDP eligibility traces gated by a global dopamine-like reward signal. Bridges unsupervised Hebbian plasticity and reinforcement learning.

Reinforcement 3-Factor Low Cost
STDP 08

Nearest-Neighbor vs All-to-All

Two strategies for selecting which spike pairs to count. All-to-all sums over every combination; nearest-neighbor uses only the most recent spikes. Critical for stability under high firing rates.

Implementation Pairing Schemes Stability
STDP 09

BCM Rule

Bienenstock-Cooper-Munro: a rate-based rule with a sliding modification threshold. Recoverable as the high-rate limit of triplet STDP. Underlies ocular dominance column formation.

Rate-Based Sliding Threshold Very Low Cost
STDP 10

Surrogate Gradient

Enables backpropagation through non-differentiable spike functions using smooth surrogate derivatives. State-of-the-art for training deep SNNs on complex benchmarks.

Supervised Deep SNN High Cost
