MODEL 04
Izhikevich Model

Two coupled equations reproducing approximately 20 distinct cortical and thalamic firing patterns by tuning four scalar parameters. Combines the computational efficiency of the LIF with the biological richness approaching Hodgkin-Huxley, making it the most widely used model in large-scale SNN research.

ODEs2
Spike PrecisionHigh
Dendritic EffectsNone
Computational CostLow
Firing Types~20

Governing Equations

Voltage & Recovery
dv/dt = 0.04v² + 5v + 140 − u + I
du/dt = a(bv − u)
SymbolDescriptionTypical
vMembrane potential (dimensionless-scaled) — state variablemV-scaled
uRecovery variable — models combined Na⁺ inactivation and K⁺ activation—
aRecovery time scale — small a = slow recovery → easier bursting0.02 (RS)
bCoupling of u to subthreshold v — higher b = stronger feedback0.2 (RS)
cAfter-spike reset value of v−65 mV
dAfter-spike increment of u — governs aftercurrent magnitude8 (RS)
IInput current (dimensionless)—

Reset Mechanism

Dual Reset
if v ≥ +30 mV : emit spike, v ← c, u ← u + d

Unlike AdEx, the reset here is purely to fixed values c and d — no ODE integration of adaptation. The simplicity is intentional: the quadratic term in dv/dt generates the spike upswing, and the reset encodes the afterhyperpolarization parametrically.

Why this matters for STDP: Firing pattern profoundly changes inter-spike interval statistics. A bursting neuron (IB) creates many rapid pre-post pairs within a burst, driving strong LTP via all-to-all STDP. A fast-spiking FS neuron creates dense spike trains that interact differently with the STDP window than adapted RS patterns.

Advantages

  • ~20 firing patterns from a single 2-ODE model with one parameter change
  • Explicitly includes the spike peak at +30 mV — more realistic ISI statistics
  • Computationally identical to LIF — scales to millions of neurons
  • Widely validated; parameters available for dozens of specific neuron types

Limitations

  • Dimensionless units — harder to connect directly to measured quantities
  • Still a point neuron — no dendritic computation
  • Quadratic term can cause numerical instability with large Δt
  • Parameters not directly interpretable as ion channel conductances
